## Proportion cautions

The drawing shows a tall ground arcade, then a two-story paired-window register, then a three-story triple register; the taller wing adds a two-story triple register. These registers matter more than making every opening the same height. Preserve current approximate footprint and building envelope for this bounded pass, but replace the current uniform repeated upper-row loop with explicit register-aware rows. If envelopes are later recalibrated from plans, keep the profiles normalized so they can be scaled consistently.

The current Lafayette entrance bay is correct: first bay of the taller wing next to the height step. Its canopy is supported by the elevation, but its exact dimensions and sculpture proportions remain interpretive.
