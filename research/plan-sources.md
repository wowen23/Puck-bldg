## Newly inspected: 2021 Torrisi landmarks submission

Source: https://cbmanhattan.cityofnewyork.us/cb2/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2021/09/275-Mulberry-St-Puck-Building-Presentation.pdf

Local research copy: `references/drawings/torrisi-2021.pdf`. Page 2 plot plan and page 5 partial ground plan rendered and visually inspected. This is an architectural application, not a certified boundary survey. It mixes existing context and proposed work.

Page 2 explicitly labels Lafayette 232.4 ft, Mulberry 257.25 ft, Jersey 120.6 ft, lot area 23,397 sq ft. The 29.75 ft and 51 ft labels identify portions associated with the work area, not full street lengths. Houston has no legible overall dimension on this sheet. These overall figures are fundamentally different from the retail leasing frontages cited in the viewer (153, 163 and 83 ft); do not use retail frontage figures as whole-building boundary dimensions.

Page 5 is a partial southern ground-floor proposed plan, not the whole building. It includes perimeter piers, column locations, stairs, elevator and existing lobby; Lafayette left, Mulberry right, Jersey bottom. It has a printed scale of 3/16 inch = 1 foot. Calibrate using a dimension or original PDF page size, not an arbitrarily resized screenshot. Proposed awning/vitrine work and tenant partitions are not original 1880s construction evidence.

Next modeling step: trace the full outer plot polygon from page 2, retain dimension labels separately, compare scale residuals on all three dimensioned sides, then cross-check the 2011 page 4 plan and CBRE shell plans. Determine the irregular construction seam from actual plans before shifting wing footprints. Do not stretch the current footprint to one frontage alone.

## CBRE core-and-shell floor plans

Source: https://thepuckbuilding.cbre-properties.com/

Page explicitly includes second- and third-floor shell plans, separate from office test fits. The image filenames exposed by the page are `2nd-entire-shell.jpg` and `3rd-entire-shell.jpg`. Web extraction of the signed CDN image links returned HTTP 403, but browser inspection successfully displayed both plans. The second-floor plan was visually checked in upper and lower portions: full tapered footprint, round column positions, Stair A on Lafayette, Stair B near Jersey, passenger and service elevator cores, and perimeter fire escapes. No readable dimensional chain was established; these sheets have not yet been used for dimensional calibration. The browser screenshot is saved locally as `research/cbre-second-floor-upper.png`. They remain a useful tracing source. Test-fit layouts should not be mistaken for existing structural plans.
