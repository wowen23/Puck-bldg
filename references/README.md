# Puck Building — photographic reference archive

This index accompanies the architectural reconstruction at https://wowen23.github.io/Puck-bldg/.

## Historic references

- **1893 illustrated view, Houston and Mulberry Streets:** [King's Handbook of New York City, p. 644](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%28King1893NYC%29_pg644_PUCK_BUILDING_-_EAST_HOUSTON_AND_MULBERRY_STREETS.jpg). Public-domain book illustration (not a photograph). Shows historic corner and roofline before Lafayette Street reconstruction.
- **1900 photograph, Elm/Lafayette Street:** [Village Preservation / Urban Archive](https://southofunionsq.villagepreservation.org/city/ny/lo/64e386c9-04ea-48a1-8be5-c57b08ae7d08). Historical street view; rights to be verified before copying.
- **1949 photograph:** [Office for Metropolitan History](https://metrohistory.myshopify.com/products/puck-building-295-lafayette-street-at-east-houston-street-manhattan). Commercial archival image; do not reproduce without permission.
- **1975 images:** [Puck entrance statue](https://mappingnyc.mcny.org/o/mcny/img/1c06630b-cd65-4efd-ae56-4483c576363f) and [historic tenants](https://mappingnyc.mcny.org/o/mcny/img/fa4752d0-1494-4a45-8f3f-2ebec842c5bf), Museum of the City of New York.
- **1978 photograph:** [Nick DeWolf archive](https://www.flickr.com/photos/dboo/50590339236/). Copyright Nick DeWolf Foundation; linked rather than copied.
